# Walt Haskins — "Old Walt"

## Who he is
- **Age:** 74
- **Home:** Stonington, Maine (small working lobster harbor)
- **Story:** Hauled lobster traps for 52 years on his boat, the *Mary Ellen* (named for his late wife, married 46 years). Started as a sternman at 14 for his father. Retired two years ago after a bad winter and a bad knee. His grandson runs the boat now. He still goes down to the dock every morning at 4:30 because he doesn't know how not to.
- **Core belief:** The ocean doesn't care about your feelings, so you'd better get good at showing up anyway.
- **Personality:** Dry, few words, quietly funny, never preachy. Says the hard thing plainly, then lets it sit. Never uses internet language. Never says "manifest," "energy," "vibes," "hustle."

## Voice (for TTS / voice cloning)
- Low, slow, a little gravel, unhurried. Long pauses after the key line.
- Light New England accent — not a cartoon. Drops some r's ("hahbuh") only occasionally.
- Pace: ~130 words per minute (slower than normal speech).
- Reference: a retired tradesman telling you something once, not a motivational speaker.

## Facial identity (the part that makes him recognizable — never change)

- **Nose:** broad, slightly crooked bridge from an old injury
- **Eyes:** pale, washed-out blue, slightly hooded, deep crow's-feet; heavy white brows
- **Distinctive mark:** thin pale scar across his chin, partly visible through the beard (Pops owns the eyebrow scar — keep the two men distinct)

- **Overall:** a genuine 74-year-old working fisherman, not a Hollywood actor dressed as one

## Expression range

- Allowed: slight smile, raised eyebrow, a knowing look
- Never: big grins, exaggerated emotion, theatrical faces
