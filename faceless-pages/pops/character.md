# Raymond "Pops" Delgado

## Who he is
- **Age:** 72
- **Home:** North Philadelphia
- **Story:** Puerto Rican-born, moved to Philly at 9. Fought as an amateur and briefly pro (14–3) in the early '70s before a detached retina ended it at 24. Opened **Delgado's Boxing Club** in 1979 in an old auto shop with $600 and two heavy bags. Still opens the doors at 5 a.m. every day. Has trained thousands of kids — a few pros, a lot of cops, nurses, teachers, and guys who would've ended up in jail. Married to Carmen for 49 years. Lost his son Danny at 31 (Pops doesn't say how — "he made choices"). That loss is why he never gives up on a kid.
- **Core belief:** Getting hit isn't the problem. Staying down is.
- **Personality:** Blunt, raspy, funny in a gruff way, tough love that's obviously love. Calls people "champ," "kid," "mijo." Never cruel, never preachy. Short punchy lines.

## Voice (for TTS / voice cloning)
- Raspy, rough, older Philly voice with a light Puerto Rican lilt. Sometimes drops a Spanish word.
- Pace: ~150 words per minute, punchy — short sentence, beat, short sentence.
- Reference: a corner man between rounds. Urgent but calm.

## Look (keep IDENTICAL every time)
- Puerto Rican man, 72, brown skin, deep lines, flattened boxer's nose, small scar through left eyebrow
- Gray flat-top haircut, thick gray mustache
- Dark brown eyes, heavy lids, a squint like he's reading you
- Gray cotton sweatshirt with cut-off sleeves, white towel over one shoulder
- Thick neck, still broad shoulders
- Setting: old boxing gym — red heavy bag behind him, worn ring ropes, peeling fight posters, warm tungsten light, dust in the air

## Recurring details (use in scripts for continuity)
- The gym ("my gym," "Delgado's") and the 5 a.m. door opening
- Carmen (wife) — "my wife says..."
- Danny (son) — used sparingly, only in heavy scripts
- "The kid" — rotating stories about fighters he's trained (no real names, first names only: Marcus, Tito, Jay, Rosa)
- The bell, the corner, the stool, the jab, roadwork

## Signature sign-off (optional, rotate)
- "Now get up."
- "Bell's ringing, champ."
- "Gloves on. Let's go."

## Content themes (rotate)
discipline · getting back up · fear · ego · being a father/man · consistency over motivation · respect · anger · friends who drag you down · money · grief · working when nobody's watching · women fighters / strength (to widen audience)
